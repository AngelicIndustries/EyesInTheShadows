Glitched sounds for the eyes in the dimensions of "glitched_sound_dimensions" (Dimensional Doors' Limbo).
All of these were made mono, run through a glitch (slices stuttered, reversed, pitch-jumped, turned to noise or cut
out, then bitcrushed and downsampled) and brought to matched loudness. Some play at a set pitch (sounds.json).
